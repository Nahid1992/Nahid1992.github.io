Band

RedRock Dreamers

RedRock Dreamers is a Phoenix, Arizona-based Bangladeshi rock band formed in 2024. The band's goal is to perform and entertain with popular Bangladeshi rock music, covering classic bands such as LRB, Miles, and Warfaze as well as newer-generation bands such as Artcell, Black, and Nemesis.

The band started with three members: Akash, Nahid, and Tomal. Encouraged by the feedback and support from the community, RedRock Dreamers hopes to grow and share its passion for rock music with even more people.

Compositions

Composing music has always been a passion of mine. After moving to the USA and leaving my band, I began creating my own instrumental compositions. I believe instrumental music tells a different story to each listener, and I enjoy hearing how different people interpret the same piece.
