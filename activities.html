Band: RedRock Dreamers

“Redrock Dreamers” is a Phoenix-AZ based Bangladeshi rock band formed in 2024. The main goal of this band is to perform and entertain with popular Bangladeshi rock music, covering not only classic band songs (e.g., LRB, Miles, Warfaze, etc.) but also relatively new generation rock band (Artcell, Black, Nemesis, etc). The band initially started with three members: Akash, Nahid, and Tomal. Based on the feedback and support from the community, the band is looking forward to expanding their talents and passion for rock music.

Compositions

Composing music has always been a passion for me. After moving to USA, quiting my band, started creating my own instrumental compositions. I believe instrumental music reflects different stories which varies from person to person. It is very interesting to hear different people's perspective for a single art.
