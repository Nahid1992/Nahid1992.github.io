About

Summary

I hold a Ph.D. in Computer Science from Arizona State University, where I developed foundation models for large-scale image analysis in collaboration with Mayo Clinic. My work focuses on Computer Vision and Deep Learning, with expertise in foundation model pretraining, multi-task learning, Vision Transformers (ViT), and Swin Transformers, building models that unify classification, detection, and segmentation within a single end-to-end framework.

I have published 6 conference and 2 journal papers with 180+ citations and h-index 7, and hold 3 granted and 5+ pending U.S. patents in AI and Deep Learning. I am a recipient of the prestigious President's Award for Innovation at Arizona State University (Dec'2024) and the SUN Award for invited research talk and subject matter expertise (Apr'2026).

Beyond academia, I completed two industry internships at Intel Corporation in Machine Learning and Computer Vision. I am experienced in large-scale experiment design, benchmarking, and optimization across diverse imaging tasks, and proficient in Python, PyTorch, and TensorFlow, with hands-on experience in distributed training on HPC/GPU clusters, large-scale data pipelines, and end-to-end model development.

Aug 2018 – May 2026Tempe, AZ×

Research Assistant

Arizona State University
ASU-Mayo Clinic Joint Research Collaboration
  • Published 8 peer-reviewed papers (6 conference, 2 journal) on Deep Learning, Computer Vision, and Image Analysis.
  • Inventor of 3 granted and 5 pending U.S. patents in AI and Deep Learning, developed in collaboration with Mayo Clinic.
  • Built scalable AI/ML frameworks on High-Performance Computing (HPC) GPU infrastructure using distributed training, Linux/SLURM-based remote computing, workflow orchestration, and large-scale data pipelines to support model training.
  • Developed a systematic benchmark comparing 12 CNN architectures, Vision/Swin Transformers, and 19 self-supervised models for image classification; proposed Embedding-based ViT (EViT), a two-stage framework that aggregates image-level representations across variable-length image sequences for volume-level prediction, improving AUC by 2.22% over prior state-of-the-art; achieved 94.3% average AUC in zero-shot transfer, with model attention verified against ground-truth regions.
  • Designed Foundation X/X+, an end-to-end multi-task foundation model framework integrating image classification, object detection, and segmentation; pretrained on 11 datasets (~826k images) spanning 11 classification, 6 detection, and 3 segmentation tasks; introduced Lock-Release Pretraining to reduce task-specific overfitting; outperformed single-task baselines across all datasets, with average gains of +1.0% AUC, +8.4% mAP, +3.7% Dice, and strong cross-task/cross-dataset generalization.
  • Developed Foundation CTPA, a unified 3D foundation model integrating image-level classification, volume-level classification, detection, and segmentation in one framework; pretrained on over 1.5 million image slices from 3D volumes; introduced Region-Guided ROI Alignment to transfer detection cues to segmentation; achieved gains over single-task baselines, averaging +6.7% image AUC, +2.6% volume AUC, +16.5% mAP, and +5.8% Dice, with strong generalization and cross-domain transferability.
